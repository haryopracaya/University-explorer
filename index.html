Global Career & Workspace

Industry landscapes expanded to include ASEAN, alongside preserved Europe & Canada data.

Singapore (NUS/NTU grads)

Focus: MedTech (Medtronic), Biotech R&D, A*STAR Institutes.

Roles: Process Engineer, Clinical Engineer, R&D Scientist.

MOE Grant requires 3-yr work in SG-registered company post-graduation.

Indonesia (Returning Grads)

Focus: Vaccine/generic drug production (Bio Farma, Kalbe, Dexa), Hospitals.

Roles: Formulation Scientist (match for gummy research!), Regulatory Affairs bridging BPOM.

Japan

Focus: Regenerative medicine, robotics, bioprocessing (Takeda, Astellas).

Roles: MSc virtually mandatory for R&D. Japanese fluency (JLPT N2/N1) critical.

Australia

Focus: Clinical trials, MedTech (Cochlear, CSL, Vaxxas).

Roles: Medical Device Engineer, Bioprocess Scientist.

Europe (NL, FI, BE)

Netherlands: MedTech (Philips) & Bio Science Parks (Leiden). BSc usually proceeds direct to MSc.

Finland: Digital health, wearables (Oura). Highly interdisciplinary (coding + wet-lab).

Belgium: High biopharma manufacturing density (Pfizer/GSK). Big demand for Bioprocess Engineers.

Canada

Focus: Strong biotech hubs integrated with public hospital networks (MaRS Toronto, UHN).

Roles: Process Engineer, Clinical Research Assistant. PhD needed for Principal Investigator roles.
