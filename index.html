Global Career & Workspace

Industry landscapes expanded to include ASEAN and Indonesian return pathways.

Singapore (NUS / NTU grads)

Industry Landscape:

Major hub for MedTech manufacturing, Biotech R&D, and Clinical Research. Government heavily funds A*STAR research institutes.

Typical Employers:
  • MedTech: Medtronic, Edwards Lifesciences, Thermo Fisher.
  • Pharma/Biotech: GSK, Sanofi.
  • Institutes: A*STAR, NUHS, SingHealth.
Roles (Progression):
  • BEng/BioEng: Process Engineer, QA/QC, Clinical Engineer (Hospital), Product Specialist.
  • MSc/PhD: R&D Scientist (A*STAR requires PhD for independent PI roles).

Note: If under MOE Tuition Grant, you must work in a Singapore-registered company for 3 years post-graduation.

Indonesia (Returning Graduates)

Industry Landscape:

Growing domestic push for health resilience (vaccines, med-devices, generics). High regulatory and hospital tech needs.

Typical Employers:
  • Pharma/Biotech: Bio Farma, Kalbe Farma, Dexa Medica.
  • Government: BRIN (National Research), BPOM, Kemenkes.
  • Hospitals: Siloam, Mitra Keluarga (Clinical Eng).
Roles:

Clinical Engineer: Managing hospital diagnostic equipment.
Formulation/R&D Scientist: Direct match for your gastroretentive/gummy research.
Regulatory Affairs: High demand bridging international products into BPOM standards.

Japan

Focus: Regenerative medicine, robotics, bioprocessing (Takeda, Astellas, Olympus, RIKEN).

Roles: MSc virtually mandatory for corporate R&D. Japanese fluency (JLPT N2/N1) critical for integration.

Australia (Brisbane/Melbourne)

Focus: Clinical trials, MedTech (Cochlear, ResMed, CSL). Vaxxas (Brisbane) for biotech delivery.

Roles: Medical Device Engineer, Bioprocess Scientist. High local competition for grads without PR.
