Career & Workspace

Workspace in Japan

Japan has a massive R&D ecosystem, particularly in pharmaceuticals, robotics, and biomaterials.

  • Industries: Pharma (Takeda, Astellas), Med-Tech (Terumo, Olympus), Biotech startups, University Labs (RIKEN).
  • Environment: Highly structured, hierarchical, heavy emphasis on continuous improvement (Kaizen). Wet labs are meticulously organized. Manufacturing roles involve strict quality control.
  • Degree Requirements: BSc is often sufficient for entry-level manufacturing/process engineering. MSc is the standard for entry into R&D / researcher roles in major companies.

Workspace in Indonesia

Indonesia is rapidly expanding its domestic healthcare manufacturing and clinical research.

  • Industries: Domestic Pharma (Kalbe Farma, Bio Farma, Dexa Medica), FMCG/Cosmetics (Paragon), Government (BRIN, BPOM), Hospital infrastructure.
  • Environment: Growing emphasis on bioprocessing, vaccine development, and formulation. More cross-functional roles (e.g., R&D blending into regulatory affairs).
  • Degree Requirements: BSc is heavily utilized in QC/QA, formulation, and clinical research associates (CRA). Overseas degrees are highly valued for leadership/management tracks.

Realistic Future Roles

R&D Formulation Scientist

Day-to-day: Wet lab. Mixing active ingredients, testing stability/release rates (like your gummy/raft-forming research). Heavy chemistry.

Progression: BSc -> MSc preferred -> Principal Scientist (PhD)

Bioprocess Engineer

Day-to-day: Manufacturing/Plant. Scaling up biological processes (fermentation, cell cultures) from lab to factory. Heavy math/physics.

Progression: BEng sufficient -> Plant Manager

Biomedical/Device Engineer

Day-to-day: CAD design, prototyping, testing materials for biocompatibility. Office + Engineering Lab.

Progression: BEng -> Senior Engineer

Clinical Research Associate

Day-to-day: Office/Hospitals. Managing clinical trials, ensuring regulatory compliance, handling data.

Progression: BSc/Life Sci -> CRA -> Clinical Trial Manager
